Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026. Your privacy and digital safety are our highest priorities. Learn how we handle information responsibly and transparently.

1. Introduction

At Smashy Road Games (accessible via https://smashy-road.github.io/), we firmly believe that playing free web games should never compromise your personal privacy. We do not require accounts, logins, credit card information, or phone numbers to play any of our titles.

This Privacy Policy outlines the minimal non-identifiable technical data collected automatically to ensure games run smoothly on your device.

2. Children's Online Privacy Protection (COPPA Compliance)

Because our web games are widely enjoyed by students in classrooms and school computer labs, we strictly adhere to the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA):

  • We do not knowingly collect, store, or solicit personally identifiable information (PII) from children under the age of 13.
  • We do not offer user profile creation, chat rooms, or public commenting features that could expose minor personal information.
  • If a parent, guardian, or teacher discovers that an underage student has submitted personal details through our contact form, please email us immediately at contact@smashy-road.github.io and we will permanently delete the information within 24 hours.

3. Technical & Analytical Information

When you access our platform, standard non-identifying server log data may be processed automatically, including:

  • Device & Browser Type: Operating system, screen resolution, browser version (used to optimize canvas rendering dimensions).
  • Referral URL: The website or search engine query that led you to our game.
  • Game Performance Metrics: Aggregate load times and frame drop statistics used to diagnose performance bottlenecks on low-spec hardware.

4. Cookies & Local Storage Usage

We use browser localStorage strictly for game convenience features:

  • Saving your in-game high score, streak records, and level progress locally on your own machine.
  • Remembering your audio volume preference (mute/unmute).

We do not use tracking cookies to build cross-site advertising profiles. You can clear your game saves and local storage at any time through your browser's settings.

5. GDPR & CCPA User Rights

Under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), users have the right to request information on any data held, request deletion, or object to processing. Because we do not store individual user accounts or personal profiles, we do not sell or monetize personal consumer records.
